Everyday actions that help
  • Check sales before restocking to avoid over-ordering.
  • Use FIFO: sell older stock first, especially items with expiry dates.
  • Separate recyclable packaging and compost suitable food scraps.
  • Switch off unused lights and equipment; fix leaking taps.
